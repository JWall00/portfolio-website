The core kids hydration line, designed to win over cautious parents and excite kids at the same time.

What I owned
Managed and developed air up's core kids product line, leading an agile, cross-functional team. Owned the research into where and how kids use bottles, where parents hit friction, and what colors, motifs, and trends genuinely excite a 6–10 audience.
Approach
A user-centric strategy that looked at the full journey for kids aged 6 to 10, and the parents buying for them. The bottle had to be something kids genuinely wanted to use and show off, because increasing hydration was the real goal. Feedback from parents later shaped an in-line design change that made the drinking experience and everyday handling even easier for small hands.
Outcome
Sold over 1.2 million bottles in its first year, expanding air up's kids and family segment to over 50% of the total customer base.

What I owned
Product lead on a first-of-its-kind bespoke line, aligning Product, Marketing, Operations, and IT. Through extensive market research and user testing, I briefed and built an expansive collection that was both operationally efficient and successful, while remaining customer-centric.
Approach

How I Work

The discipline behind how I take a product from first idea to launch day.

Every project starts with the identification of a user need not being met. I dive further into market and trend research, user and persona insights, and competitive context. I turn that into a clear product vision, positioning, and concept, grounded in data and discoveries, so the direction is validated before development begins.

Market & trend research · User & persona insight · Positioning & concept · Business analytics

I translate strategy into roadmaps, timelines, and milestones, then run delivery with the given team. That has meant working in agile sprints as well as more traditional cross-departmental, stage-gated/waterfall developments. I own the product data, specs, and backlog, so priorities stay clear and everyone knows what to build next.

Roadmapping · Timeline & milestone planning · Agile / scrum · Backlog & specs · Product data

I lead cross-functional teams across Design, Insights, Operations, Marketing, and IT, keeping stakeholders aligned throughout the creation process. In the past, I've co-owned the go-to-market plan and managed the vendors and partners to ensure we get the product out the door. As a PM, alignment is key to bringing products to life successfully.
